GREEN LIVING — SINGLE KLING CINEMATIC HERO

- Replaced the six-clip hero playback sequence with the supplied Kling video at public/videos/green-living-journey.mp4.
- The hero video does not autoplay. It starts on its first frame.
- GSAP ScrollTrigger pins the hero while wheel scrolling scrubs the complete video.
- The same scroll progress drives a camera-style scale animation (strong seedling push-in, then a gradual settle through the environments).
- Scrolling upward reverses both video time and zoom.
- After the final frame, the pin releases and the original Green Living homepage sections continue in normal document flow.
- Existing navigation, CTA, shop, studio, dashboard, customizer and plant-care functionality remain in the project.
- This source project is vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript, so GSAP is integrated without converting/rebuilding it in React.

Run: npm start, then open http://localhost:3000.
GSAP/ScrollTrigger are loaded from jsDelivr and require internet access; the MP4 is local.
