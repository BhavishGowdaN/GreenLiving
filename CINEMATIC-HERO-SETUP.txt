GREEN LIVING — CINEMATIC SCROLL HERO

Run locally:
1. Install Node.js 20+ if it is not already installed.
2. Open a terminal in this GreenLiving folder.
3. Run: npm start
4. Open: http://localhost:3000

Hero behavior:
- The Kling MP4 is assets/videos/green-living-journey.mp4.
- Scroll down to advance through the video; scroll up to rewind.
- The hero remains pinned until the video reaches its final frame.
- After the final frame, the existing INTRO section immediately below the hero appears.
- The original site sections and interactions remain in place.

GSAP and ScrollTrigger are loaded from jsDelivr in index.html, so an internet connection is required for the scroll pinning library.
